# 🎄 Jingle Quest: The Great Hush

**A Christmas LitRPG for kids ages 8–12.** 

> It's Christmas Eve at the North Pole. The Jingle Engine just went KA-SPLONG, five glowing gears are scattered across the land, and a lonely villain called Baron Von Hush is stealing every bell in the world. Grab your gingerbread sidekick Nutmeg and save Christmas.


## ✨ Full description

Jingle Quest is a story-driven, turn-based role-playing game with dice-roll skill checks, boss battles, loot, a party, mini-games, and a branching ending. Every enemy can be defeated **or befriended**. Kindness is a real game mechanic, and the best ending is earned by being kind to the villain.

### The story: 12 chapters in 3 acts, plus a finale
| Act | Chapters |
|---|---|
| **I. The Scattered Gears** | Gumdrop Forest · Toy Factory · Frostfin Lake · Cookie Kitchen · Candy Cane Peak |
| **II. Beyond the Pole** | Aurora Fields · Peppermint Mines · Snow Globe Village · Wrapping Warehouse |
| **III. The Great Hush** | Silent Woods · Sleigh Graveyard · Cathedral of Ice |
| **Finale** | Christmas Eve: the Sleigh Flight and the endings |

Collect **5 Jingle Gears + 7 Silver Bells = 12 quest pieces**. Act I ends on a cliffhanger.

### Heroes
Pick one of four classes. Each has four special moves unlocked at levels 1, 4, 9, and 15.
- 🦌 **Reindeer Rider**: tough, fast, hits hard
- ❄️ **Snow Mage**: sparkle magic and shields
- 🧝 **Sneaky Elf**: traps, critical hits, and stealing goodies
- 🍪 **Cookie Alchemist**: healing, bombs, and friendship powers

Four stats: **Might, Sparkle, Sneak, Kindness**. Spend a point every level, up to level 20.

### Battles
- Enemies **telegraph their next move**, so kids can plan (Guard before a BIG hit!)
- Weakness bonuses, critical hits, and a **Jolly Meter** that unlocks a **Merry Burst**
- **Two ways to win:** empty the Grump bar, or fill the **Friendship hearts** with Cheer Up! and gifts
- Multi-phase bosses, and a friendly retreat instead of "game over"
- Rematch bosses any time for loot

### Loot & collecting
- Gear in 5 slots with 4 rarities (Common → Legendary), perks, and 12 unique boss drops
- **Cookie Forge**: craft goodies and upgrade gear to +5
- **7 companions** to befriend (Fluff, Sniffles, Figgy, Grimble, Dash, Tink, Pip), each with a passive bonus and a once-per-battle assist
- **12 Golden Ornaments** for the Ornament Tree, with tier bonuses
- **24-door Advent Calendar**, 25 badges, chapter stars
- **Blizzard Mode** (New Game+) for replay

### Mini-games
Crystal Tap and other timing games · Bell Choir (memory) · Gift Catch · Sleigh Flight runner. Each one pays out 0–3 stars.
